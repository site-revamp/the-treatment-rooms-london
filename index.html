The Treatment Rooms approach

Expertise, with the individual at the centre.

A specialist procedure deserves personal attention at every stage — from the first discussion to treatment planning and aftercare.

“A thoughtful plan begins with understanding the person, not simply the procedure.”

The Treatment Rooms is a specialist hair restoration clinic in Putney, London. Founded by surgeons Dr Roshan Vara and Dr Dilan Fernando, the clinic places surgeon involvement at the centre of its approach.

Rather than treating every patient as the same case, the aim is to understand individual hair loss patterns, expectations and suitability before discussing possible treatment pathways.
