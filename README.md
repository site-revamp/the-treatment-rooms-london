# The Treatment Rooms London – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

The Treatment Rooms London is a surgeon-led hair restoration clinic based in Putney, London, providing personalised surgical hair restoration and hair loss care for male, female and transgender patients.

The redesigned homepage presents the clinic as a specialist provider focused on natural-looking results, direct surgeon involvement and individual patient care. Its structure is designed to help prospective patients understand the clinic’s treatments, expertise and consultation process more easily.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Updated and Consistent Trust Signals
The homepage gives greater priority to the clinic’s credibility and professional recognition. Award information is presented more clearly to address inconsistencies between the homepage’s 2026 awards and the older 2023 recognition referenced on the About Us page. This helps create a more coherent and current presentation of the clinic.

### 2. Simplified Homepage Content and Structure
Overlapping information about treatments, surgeon expertise, awards, results and aftercare has been organised into clearer content sections. Repetitive promotional messaging has been reduced, making the clinic’s services and key differentiators easier to identify.

### 3. Clearer Consultation and Contact Journey
The consultation call to action and telephone contact option receive clearer prominence. The revised presentation is intended to make the next step easier to understand and reduce uncertainty around submitting an enquiry and waiting for a response.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual hierarchy:** Important clinic information, treatment categories and consultation actions are given clearer prominence.
- **Content organisation:** Information is grouped into more focused sections to improve scanning and navigation.
- **Reduced repetition:** Overlapping promotional content is streamlined to create a more focused homepage.
- **Brand presentation:** The layout supports a more consistent presentation of the clinic’s surgeon-led positioning and patient-care approach.
- **User experience:** The page guides prospective patients from understanding the clinic and its treatments towards making an enquiry.

## Project Objective

Create a clearer, more consistent homepage that communicates The Treatment Rooms London’s specialist hair restoration services, strengthens its presentation of credibility and makes the consultation journey easier for prospective patients to follow.
